3 / Samples & mix

Library
About banks, sounds and recording

The bank shown here is the channel selected in the step sequencer: click a channel's name on the grid (or pick it above) to see its sounds. Click a sound's name to hear it and see its waveform. The round button picks the sound the channel plays; checked sounds form the pool for Cycle, Random and per-hit sounds (Alt-click a hit, or press V, to give it its own sound). With Random, each step keeps its pick on every loop so exports match playback; tick New pick every loop to re-roll on each pass. 🎲 on a card picks new sounds and re-voices that bank's built-ins. To add a channel, use + Channel in the step sequencer toolbar.

Timing: Lock to grid ignores swing and humanize; Snap moves the channel's hits to the nearest 1/8 or 1/4 as they play (the grid you drew stays the same); Gate cuts each hit after a set length, or when the channel's next hit starts (Choke); Tempo-sync repitches long samples such as breaks to fill whole beats at the current tempo (Auto picks 1, 2, 4… beats).

Built-in sounds are synthesized in the browser: 808, 909 and crushed breakcore-style emulations, not recordings. Open a sample pack folder (or drop one on this panel) to browse it, subfolders included; click a sound to hear it, then click a channel to add it there, or the lit channel again to take it out. The dashed channel is the guess from its file and folder names. Drop files on the card to load them into that channel. Files stay in your browser.

Resize this panel to see more or less of the sound list: drag its left edge to make it wider or narrower, and its bottom edge to make it taller or shorter (or focus an edge and use the arrow keys). Double-click an edge to reset it; with no height set, the panel matches the step sequencer's height. Your browser remembers the size.

The library meter shows how much memory loaded sounds use. The library holds up to 10,000 sounds and 8 GB of decoded audio. Loaded sounds are kept decoded so they play on time, at about 375 KB per second of stereo audio whatever the file size on disk, so long loops and breaks fill it much faster than one-shots. Sounds over the limit are skipped (the message says how many); Remove loaded, or ✕ on a sound, frees the memory. Each file can be up to 50 MB and 60 seconds long.

Clear banks starts over with the standard channels (Kick, Snare, Clap, Open Hat, Closed Hat, Crash, Ride, Shaker, Rim, Tom, Sub, Other), each with its built-in sounds, and a blank pattern; Full clear does the same with completely empty banks. Loaded samples stay in the library, so the pack browser can add them again, and Undo puts everything back.

● Record (top bar) captures exactly what you hear at the Rec level (after the limiter, without the metronome; Play volume doesn't affect it), including knob moves, Mangle variations and hits you play while it runs. It starts at the loop start bar (starting playback there if it is stopped) and keeps looping until you press it again; the loop then finishes, playback stops, and a WAV cut on the loop boundaries is saved. Pressing Stop or Pause saves what was recorded so far. Recordings are limited to 30 minutes.

About Mangle

Mangle plans effects on the 16th-note grid, so everything stays locked to tempo and swing. Loop rolls repeat a slice at 1/16 to 1/64; chops replay another beat of the pattern, sometimes reversed or pitched; reverse swells rise into a snare; delay throws send hits to a tempo-synced echo; reverb throws send hits into the Reverb below (they work even when the Reverb is switched off). Tape stops slow the beat down to a halt at the end of a phrase, and spin-ups bring it back up to speed into the next beat. Planned effects appear in the FX row of the grid, dashed. You can also place effects yourself: choose one under FX row adds in the step sequencer toolbar, with a length, and click the FX row. Your effects play whether Mangle is on or off, stay put when Mangle picks a new variation, and Mangle plans its random effects around them. Click one of Mangle's effects to keep it as yours; click (or right-click) one of yours to remove it. Each variation number always produces the same plan, so you can come back to one you like.

FX rack Phaser → Delay → Reverb, on everything the pattern plays

About the FX rack and knobs

Tick an effect's name to switch it on. 🎲 gives one effect new settings; Lock keeps an effect as it is when you use Randomize all effects or Randomize all. Delay and Phaser times follow the tempo. Switching an effect off lets its echoes and tail ring out instead of cutting them.

Knobs: drag up or down to turn (hold Shift for fine moves), double-click to reset to the default. Click a knob, then use the arrow keys, Page Up / Page Down, Home / End or the mouse wheel.

5 / Pattern files & export options

About exports and pattern files

Export WAV and Export MIDI sit at the top of the step sequencer, with the export range beside them. Exports include only audible lanes (respecting mute and solo), with swing, humanize and the first Mangle variation applied. WAV exports include the FX rack, with tails wrapped back to the start when seamless loop is on. MIDI uses General MIDI drum notes on channel 10, one track per channel: kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42, other 46, and added channels use the note set on their card; reverse swells, pitch, delay, reverb and the FX rack are audio-only. Pattern files save the sequence, settings and which sounds are checked, not the audio itself; reopen the same pack folder to bring back the sounds you used.

Break Lab is a local browser tool. Reference grooves are editable rhythmic approximations, not extracted recordings, and the built-in drum sounds are synthesized emulations; use your own legally obtained audio for sample content.
